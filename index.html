Uzay Gökay

I am a PhD student at Centre for AI and Neuroscience at the University of Bonn, supervised by Dominik Bach and Jürgen Gall. Previously, I completed my Master degree in Life Science Informatics at the University of Bonn, where I worked on biomedical text mining at Fraunhofer SCAI under the supervision of Holger Fröhlich and completed a research internship at Bayer.

My current research focuses on human motion understanding. More specifically, I work on skeleton-based temporal action segmentation in unsupervised and self-supervised learning settings.
